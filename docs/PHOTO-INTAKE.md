# The Photo inbox and the Photo library

For Dr. Ali and the staff. How the day's patient photos get into the right patient's file with a proper name, edited, without anyone
typing a name or an Mr#.

## 1. What it does

Take the patients' photos all day as usual. After the last patient, open **Photos → Photo inbox**, choose the city folder (Karachi,
Lahore or Islamabad) and choose **all** of the day's photos at once. The website reads the time each photo was taken, compares it with the
patients' times on that city's Aaj ki List (checked in, in treatment, completed) and puts each patient's photos into their file by itself
when it is clear. When it is not clear, the photos wait on **"Which patient?"** with the two or three likely patients: one tap on the
right one files them. Nothing is ever filed by guessing. Claude (Anthropic's AI) then turns each photo upright, flips mirror shots, levels
and crops it and evens out the light, and every photo gets a proper name such as
**"Ayesha Khan - Mr# 50013 - Month 6 - Front - 17-10-2026.jpg"**. All edited photos are also in **Photos → Photo library**, where they
can be searched and downloaded. The originals are always kept exactly as they were taken (staff only).

Who may: **assistants, the clinic coordinator and Dr. Ali** use the Photo inbox ("Use the Photo inbox" in the access list; it also
needs "View raw clinic photos folder"). Moving a filed set to another patient and "Undo filing" need "Delete clinic photos and X-rays"
(assistants, the coordinator, Dr. Ali). Everyone who opens patient profiles sees the Photo library (edited photos); originals only with
"View raw clinic photos folder". The front desk keeps Upload picture / Upload X-ray on the patient profile as before.

## 2. Taking the photos

- **The phone's date, time and time zone must be automatic** (Settings → Date and time → Automatic). The time on the photo is what finds
  the patient. The upload screen shows each phone's last photo time ("iPhone 13: 14 photos, the last at 9:10 PM (now 9:30 PM)"): if that
  is wrong, the phone's clock is wrong. A phone whose clock runs ahead, or that is on another time zone, is noticed: all of its photos of
  that upload then wait on "Which patient?".
- Take each patient's photos together, one patient after the other. **Wait a few minutes between two patients** (photos less than 3
  minutes apart on one phone count as one sitting).
- **Two phones of the same model** (two assistants with the same Samsung): each phone's photos are told apart when each person uploads
  their own phone, or one person uploads the phones one after the other (two uploads). Do not put two such phones' photos on a laptop
  and upload them together in one go.
- **Mark patients "In treatment" and "Completed" on time on the Aaj ki List.** The time the patient went into treatment (or was checked
  in) is what the photo time is compared with. Statuses marked late in one go at the end of the day cannot be used: those photos then
  wait on "Which patient?".
- **iPhone:** Settings → Camera → Formats → **Most Compatible** (JPEG). HEIC photos ("High Efficiency") are uploaded and kept, but only
  Safari (iPhone or Mac) can edit them.
- Photographing a patient twice in one visit (before and after) is fine: you are asked which set is which.

## 3. Uploading (once, at the end of the day)

1. **Photos → Photo inbox.**
2. Choose the **city folder** where the patients were (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad).
3. Karachi has three branches: if all the photos are from one branch, **choose it** under "Which branch were these taken at?" (an
   assistant who works at one branch has it chosen already). With **"All Karachi branches"** a set is filed by itself only when nobody
   was in the chair at another Karachi branch at that time; otherwise it asks (the system cannot know which branch the photos are from).
   So the coordinator and Dr. Ali get more photos filed by themselves when they upload one branch at a time.
4. Press **Choose photos** and select all of the day's photos **from the phone's gallery (Photos / Gallery app), not from WhatsApp**.
   WhatsApp removes the time the photo was taken. On a laptop you can also drag the photos onto the page.
5. Wait for "uploaded" and keep the page open while it works. It says how many sets were filed by themselves and how many wait on
   "Which patient?".
- If the wifi drops, the page tries again by itself (after 2, 5, 15, 30 and 60 seconds). If something still did not finish, simply choose
  the same photos again: nothing is uploaded twice ("Already uploaded"), and a photo that failed in the middle of a sitting joins the
  sitting that was filed.
- Uploading the next morning is fine: photos go to the day they were taken.
- A second upload the same day (another phone) is fine too: everything of that day is matched again together.
- **30, 50 or 100 photos in one go is fine.** The page reads them one by one ("Reading photo 12 of 50…"), uploads three at a time and shows
  "Uploading 12 of 50 (48 MB of 160 MB)". Keep the page open until it says how many sets were filed.

## 3a. The note box ("Details about these photos")

Above **Choose photos** there is a small box: **Details about these photos (optional): patient name, Mr#, month...** Type in it
**before** choosing the photos; it belongs to the photos chosen in that upload, and is shown with them on "Which patient?" and on Filed.
It is optional: without it, everything works as before.

- **An Mr#** (for example "Ayesha Khan Mr# 50013 month 6"): when the note names exactly **one** Mr#, that patient is on the city's Aaj ki
  List that day, and the photos of the upload are one visit (taken within 2 hours), the photos go **straight into that patient's file**,
  even when the time alone could not tell (two photo-month patients in the chair). The Filed list says "Automatic: the note with these
  photos names Mr# 50013, on the DHA list that day".
- **Names, or several Mr#s**: those patients come **first** on "Which patient?" (marked "Mr# in the note" or "name in the note"). One tap
  still files. **A name alone never files photos by itself** (two patients can share a name).
- If the note names an Mr# but the photo time fits **another** photo-month patient, nothing is filed by itself: the card says so and you
  choose. If the Mr# is not on that day's list, the card says so: add the patient to the Aaj ki List and press Check again, or use
  "Search all patients".
- What is read: an Mr# is a number of 3 to 6 digits (50013, 9815, 025) or an old one like 347-1. Phone numbers, dates (17-10-2026) and
  years (2025) are not Mr#s. "Month 6", "M6" or "6th month" is the braces month (used for old photos, below).
- A whole day's photos uploaded together with a note about one patient: the note only puts that patient first on the cards; it files
  nothing by itself (the photos are more than one visit).

## 3b. Old photos (already edited)

For the clinic's old photos that were edited before (about 2,000, on phones and laptops, in no particular folder):

1. **Photos → Photo inbox → Upload**, the city folder, then **Old photos (already edited)** under "What are you uploading?".
2. **One patient per upload works best: choose that patient's photos, type the Mr# in the box, upload. Then the next patient.** For example
   type "Sana Farooq Mr# 9820 Month 6", then Choose photos and pick only her photos. The box empties itself after each upload, ready for
   the next patient.
3. With exactly one Mr# that a patient has, the photos are **filed to that patient straight away** (no visit is needed), named with the
   month of the note ("Month 6"), or "Old photo" when the note has no month. Filed says "Automatic: old photos; the note with them names
   Mr# 9820".
4. Otherwise they wait on **"Which patient?"** as one card per upload (photos of one upload taken more than 3 minutes apart make separate
   cards): the patients the note names are big buttons (by Mr#, this city's patients first, then names), or **search all patients** by name
   or Mr# on the card. A note listing several Mr#s ("50 patients at a time") offers all of them on every card of that upload: tap the right
   one for each card.

What happens to old photos:
- They keep **their own date** (the date in the photo, else the day they are uploaded), however old. They are **not matched by time** with
  the Aaj ki List and the branch question is not asked.
- They are **not sent to Claude** (they are edited already, and it costs nothing). The original is kept as it is (staff only), and an
  "as it is" copy (upright, at most 2048 pixels, nothing else changed) is what the patient file, the portal and the Photo library show.
- The patient sees them 24 hours after filing, like every Photo inbox photo (Hide / Move / Undo filing work the same). Filed shows the day
  of the photos (the day picker shows older years too).
- iPhone HEIC photos chosen in a browser other than Safari are uploaded and filed with their original, but the "as it is" copy (what the
  patient sees) is made later by the inbox's editing on a Safari page (iPhone or Mac); that editing runs once the AI key is set up (Claude
  is still not used for old photos). Easiest: choose old photos as JPEG (most edited photos are JPEG already).

## 3c. A picture and a text box with each photo

After you choose the photos, the list under the upload shows **every photo as a small picture**, with its file name, how far it has got
("Ready", "Uploading", "Uploaded") and **its own small box: "Details: name, Mr#, month"**. The pictures appear one by one while the page
reads the photos (50 or 100 photos are fine on a phone or a laptop). An iPhone HEIC photo outside Safari shows a plain tile with its name
instead of a picture: it is uploaded all the same.

- Type in a photo's box **whenever you like**: before the upload starts, while it runs, or after it says "uploaded". It is saved with that
  photo by itself ("Saved" under the box). If the wifi drops, it says "Not saved yet" and tries again by itself; keep the page open until
  it says "Saved". A box typed for a photo that did not finish uploading is kept: choose the same photos again and it comes back.
- Use it for what that photo is or who it is: "Ayesha Khan Mr# 50013 month 6", "front", "after debond". It is read **exactly like the
  note box above** (3a), and both can be used together: the upload's note is about all the photos chosen, a photo's box about that photo.
- **An Mr#** in a photo's box: the photos of that sitting go **straight into that patient's file** when, over the whole set, the boxes and
  the upload's note name exactly **one** Mr#, one patient has it, and (for today's photos) the patient is on that day's Aaj ki List of the
  city. A photo's own box does not need the "one visit" rule of the upload's note: it is about that photo.
- **Two different Mr#s** in one set (two photos, or a photo and the upload's note): nothing is filed by itself; "Which patient?" offers
  both. Photos whose boxes name different Mr#s are never put in one set: a new set starts at the photo with the other Mr#.
- **A name alone never files.** It puts that patient first on "Which patient?".
- If a photo's box names another patient than the one the photo time fits, nothing is filed by itself: the card says so and you choose.
- The box is shown **under its picture** on "Which patient?" and on Filed. After a set is filed, changing a box only changes what is
  shown: it never moves the photos (use "Move to another patient").
- The boxes are for the staff only: patients never see them.

## 4. The screens

**Which patient?** One card per set of photos that is not clear. The card shows the photos (tap one to see it large), the day, the
time, how many photos, the camera, **why it waits** (see section 5) and the likely patients as big buttons: name, Mr#, branch, month
("Month 10 (photo month)") and when they were in the chair. **One tap on the right patient files the photos.**
- **Someone else…**: search the day's list of that city by name, Mr# or token; **Search all patients** for a patient who is not on that
  day's list.
- **Not a patient photo**: puts the set aside (it is kept).
- **Split this set**: when one set has two patients, tap "Next patient starts here" on the first photo of the second patient.
- **Move to another city folder**: when the photos were put in the wrong city.
- **Check these** (at the top): filed sets where Claude thinks the photos show more than one patient, or that one photo is not a patient
  photo. Their edited photos are held back from the patient until someone presses **It's fine**, moves them or undoes the filing. Also a
  filed set whose patient record was removed (it says so): move it to the right patient.
- A set **without a camera time** has no likely patients (its day cannot be trusted): type the patient's name or Mr# in the search that
  is open on the card.
- **Check again**: matches again now (for example after a missing patient was added to the Aaj ki List). This also happens by itself
  every 3 minutes while the Photo inbox is open.

**Filed**: the sets filed on a day, with the patient (a link to their file), how they were filed ("Automatic: the only photo-month patient
in the chair at 6:35 PM (DHA)" or "Confirmed by ..."), the editing state and what the patient sees. Buttons: **Hide from patient** / **Show
to patient**; **Try Claude again** (when a photo only got a basic edit); **Move to another patient…**; **Undo filing** (the photos go
back to "Which patient?"; they are kept); **Take one photo out**. **Delete** on the patient's profile also takes that photo out of its set
(it goes to Set aside and never comes back into the file by itself).

**Set aside**: photos marked "Not a patient photo" or taken out of a filed set. **Put back** sends them to "Which patient?". Nothing is
ever deleted.

The number on **Photos** in the menu (and on Today) is how many sets wait on "Which patient?" plus the ones to check.

## 5. When it files by itself, and why it sometimes asks

It files a set by itself only when **all** of this holds: every photo has its camera time and the phone's clock looks right; the set is
one sitting from one phone and one upload (not more than 20 photos within 15 minutes); the patient is a **photo-month** patient of that
city and day who was **in treatment or completed**, with a real start or check-in time that the photos fit; **no other photo-month (or
bonding / braces-off) patient was in the chair then, even one who already has photos**; without a chosen branch, nobody was in the chair
at another branch then; the patient has no photos yet; and no other set of the day points to the same patient. Everything else asks.

What it does **not** check (a choice, to keep most sets automatic): another patient of the **same branch** in the chair at the same time
whose visit is not a photo month and not bonding / braces off (a Scaling or a normal monthly visit). Photos are taken at photo months, so
the photo-month patient is taken to be the one photographed. If a non-photo-month patient was photographed less than 3 minutes after a
photo-month patient on the same phone, both go into one set and into the photo-month patient's file; once the AI key is added, Claude
notices two different patients in one set and holds the edited copies back. Until then: wait a few minutes between two patients, and
look at the Filed list once a day (the patient sees nothing for 24 hours).

## 6. Editing with Claude

While any Photo inbox page is open, Claude looks at a small copy of each photo (1024 pixels, with no name, no Mr#, no date: only the
picture) and says what it shows (front, right side, left side, upper arch, lower arch, overjet, close-up, face front, face smile,
profiles, X-ray, document) and how to turn, flip (mirror shots), straighten and crop it, and whether it is too dark or tinted. The
website then makes the edited copy from the original: upright, mirror shots flipped, levelled, cropped, gentle brightness and colour
(never more than 8 % on any colour, so **teeth are never whitened** and before/after photos stay comparable), at most 2048 pixels. If
Claude declines a photo, or its answer cannot be read three times, a basic edit is made (brightness only) and the Filed list offers **Try
Claude again**. A long set goes to Claude in several parts; each part also shows Claude the last photo of the part before, so two patients
in one set are noticed there too. The original is never changed.

If the connection drops, Anthropic is busy, or there is a rate limit, the page simply waits and tries again (nothing is counted against
the photos). If the key is refused or **the Anthropic account has no credit left**, editing stops and the page says so: no photo is edited
badly meanwhile; fix it and reload the page.

**The patient sees the edited photos in their portal 24 hours after they were made** (filed, or edited later: when Claude edits a photo
days after it was filed, the 24 hours start then), so staff can check Claude's edit, hide, move or undo first.

Until the AI key is added the page says **"Editing waits for the AI key"**: the photos are uploaded and filed all the same, and Claude
edits them as soon as the key is there (nothing needs uploading again).

## 7. The Photo library

**Photos → Photo library**: every patient's edited photos. Search by name or Mr#; filter by city, branch, month, view and dates. Tap a
photo to see it large, **Download** it (it saves under its proper name) or open the patient's file. Tick photos (or "Select all shown", or
the box next to a patient's day) and press **Download selected (zip)** for one zip file with all the proper names (at most 300 photos and
500 MB: about 1,000 edited photos, or 120 originals).
Staff with "View raw clinic photos folder" can switch to the originals.

## 8. The AI key (Dr. Ali, yourself; never send it in a chat)

1. Go to **console.anthropic.com** and sign in (or create the clinic's account; add a payment method under Billing).
2. **API keys → Create key**, name it "clinic photos", and copy it.
3. Go to the **Supabase dashboard → your project → Edge Functions → Secrets** (Manage secrets) → **Add new secret**:
   name **ANTHROPIC_API_KEY**, value = the key → **Save**.
4. Open the Photo inbox: editing starts on the photos already uploaded.

To stop AI editing, delete that secret. If the page says **"the AI key was refused"**, the key is wrong or was removed: make a new one.
If it says **"the Anthropic account has no credit left"**: console.anthropic.com → Billing → add credit, then reload the Photo inbox.

## 9. Cost and storage

- **Claude editing**: about **1 US cent per photo** (about $0.075 for a sitting of 8 photos). 25 photos a day ≈ **$6 a month**; 100 a day
  ≈ **$25 a month** (say $5-35 with retries). Dr. Ali and accounts see "AI editing this month" at the bottom of the Photo inbox, with the
  cost by month; the bill itself is at console.anthropic.com → Usage.
- **Storage** (Supabase): the original as taken (~4 MB), the edited copy (~0.5 MB, at most 2048 px, JPEG 85 %) and two small copies
  (320 px, ~25 kB each): about **4.6 MB per photo**. 25 photos a day ≈ **3 GB a month**; 100 a day ≈ **12 GB a month**. Supabase Pro
  includes **100 GB** (about 8 months at 100 photos a day, 2.5 years at 25), then about $0.021 per GB a month (150 GB ≈ $1 a month extra).
- **Spend cap**: with Supabase's spend cap on, uploads stop once the 100 GB are used. The inbox then says "The clinic's photo storage is
  full" and keeps everything already uploaded. Before about 90 GB: Supabase dashboard → Organization → Billing / Usage → turn the spend
  cap off (or check the usage).
